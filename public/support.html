Common questions

How do I delete my data?

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete my diver & data. It removes your diver from our server — your stats, species, friends, room history and trades — and wipes this phone. It is permanent. If you cannot use the button, email us with your diver name and we will do it for you.

Someone is bothering me

Open the Dive Club, tap the diver, and choose Block or Report. Blocking hides you from each other everywhere in Reefy, cancels any trade between you, and takes effect immediately — you do not have to wait for us. You can undo it in Settings → Blocked divers. Reporting sends it to us to review.

I paid for Pro but it isn’t showing

Tap Restore purchases on the paywall or in Settings → Subscription. Purchases belong to the Apple ID that paid, so restore works after reinstalling or on a new phone — as long as you are signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. Go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or use Settings → Cancel subscription in Reefy, which opens the same place. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next renewal.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, since Apple takes the payment. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. Tell us too if something in the app caused it — we would rather fix the cause.

My dive didn’t count

With Deep Focus on, leaving the app mid-dive ends the dive and loses the catch — that is the mechanic, not a bug. Without it, dives complete normally. If a finished dive genuinely did not register, it may have been queued while you were offline; reopen the app with a connection and it should sync. If it still doesn’t, email us.

Does Reefy block other apps?

No. Reefy cannot block other apps and does not claim to. Deep Focus only detects that you left, and ends the dive.
